Potential ARIA Startup Flow
Layer 1 — Tone Establishment

Very casual.

Not:

Please complete your profile.

More like:

What are you mainly hoping ARIA helps you improve right now?

Then selectable vibes:

Focus
Routine
Productivity
Fitness
Creativity
Organization
Recovery
Balance
Learning
Custom

This frames intent without over-defining identity.

Layer 2 — Context Domains

This is where your:

business mind vs personal mind

idea becomes smart.

Because humans operate in modes.

ARIA could ask:

Which parts of life should ARIA actively learn from?

Toggleable domains:

Work/Business
School
Health/Fitness
Social
Finance
Creativity
Entertainment
Daily Routine

This gives:

boundaries.

Very important for privacy and personalization.

Layer 3 — Behavioral Calibration

Instead of asking:

“Who are you?”

Ask:

“How do you usually operate?”

Examples:

Are your days structured or spontaneous?
Do you prefer reminders or passive suggestions?
Morning productivity or night productivity?
Do you multitask often?
What usually disrupts your routines?

These are:

adaptive behavior questions,

not personality quizzes.

That aligns much better with ARIA’s philosophy.

Layer 4 — Passive Learning Phase

This is where ARIA becomes unique.

After onboarding:

ARIA should mostly observe quietly.

Not aggressively interrupt.

Maybe:

detect routines,
identify repeated actions,
infer behavioral clusters,
build temporal understanding.

Then slowly begin:

recommendations,
reminders,
predictive suggestions.
Key design philosophy:

ARIA should feel like:

a system learning your rhythm,
not:
an AI interrogating your soul.

That distinction matters psychologically.

Another VERY important thing:
Transparency Layer

Since ARIA is behavioral:
users should understand:

what’s being tracked,
what’s local,
what’s inferred,
what’s optional.

This becomes a HUGE trust advantage.

Example startup tone

Honestly something like this could work:

ARIA adapts best over time.

You don’t need to define yourself perfectly now.
Just point me toward the parts of life you want help improving.

That already feels:

intelligent,
non-invasive,
adaptive,
and human-centered.

Most successful products launch WAY smaller than people imagine.

You do NOT need:
